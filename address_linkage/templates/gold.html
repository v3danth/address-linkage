Gold set: who lives near whom

Keys: 1 same house · 2 neighbour · 3 not near · 4 can't tell · arrow keys move
Same house: the same home, i.e. same house, flat or building number on the same street (family or flatmates). Neighbour: a different home but close: same street or lane, same building or complex, or the same small colony or village. Not near: a different area. Can't tell: an address too vague to decide.
Judge only from the addresses. Every candidate needs a label; the list may contain nobody near. Local file with personal data: do not share.
